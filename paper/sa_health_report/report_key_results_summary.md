# Key Results Summary

- Best epidemiological strategy in current illustrative outputs: Prevention-targeted.
- Best economic strategy by cost per DALY averted: Prevention-targeted.
- Best economic strategy by cost per Dale-compatible mortality-adjusted QALY gained: Prevention-targeted.
- Current results are cost-saving under included costs, but this depends on unresolved programme setup, running, outreach, travel, staff time, clinical review, active TB exclusion, and false-positive incremental costs.
- Key sensitivity caveats: TB CFR, YLL per TB death, mortality-adjusted QALY assumptions, and local programme delivery costs require SA Health / APY review.
- Post-TB sensitivity impact for prevention-targeted screening: DALYs increase from 19.8 to 89.4; QALYs increase from 15.8 to 27.0.
- Recommendation for review: treat the model as a planning and sequencing tool, review unresolved costs and mortality/post-TB assumptions, and preserve open access to clinically indicated screening and treatment.
