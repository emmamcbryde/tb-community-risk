## Why Cost-Saving Occurs

Current included programme costs are testing plus preventive treatment. Programme setup/running, outreach, travel, staff time, follow-up support, and false-positive incremental costs are currently zero. At AUD $19,080 per active TB case, each strategy breaks even at roughly 3 prevented active TB cases. The generated outputs prevent 4 to 12 cases, so avoided TB disease costs exceed included programme costs.
