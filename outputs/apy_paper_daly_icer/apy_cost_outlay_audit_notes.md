# APY Cost-Outlay Audit Notes

The current cost-saving result is driven by active TB disease costs averted exceeding the currently included programme costs.

Currently included cost components are screening test costs and preventive treatment course costs. For the base-case APY paper outputs, this means IGRA at AUD 113.48 per person screened and 3HP at AUD 165.5072 per TPT course started. Active TB disease costs averted use AUD 19,079.60 per active TB case prevented.

The current outputs do not fully include programme setup/running costs, outreach, travel, staff time, result return, clinic visits, active TB exclusion work-up such as CXR/lab work, follow-up support, adherence support, or false-positive incremental management costs unless these are supplied separately. These missing costs are why the cost-saving result should be described as conditional on currently included costs.

Dale et al. used a richer care pathway and a different population, with an onshore Australian migrant setting and lifetime Markov framing. The APY analysis is a higher-burden closed cohort with direct person-level prevented cases, no emigration, and no downstream transmission benefit. These structural and population differences can explain why the current APY base case appears cost-saving while Dale et al. did not find the same conclusion in the migrant setting.

Final report wording should say that APY strategies are cost-saving under currently included costs, and should explicitly state that adding APY-specific programme delivery costs could reduce or remove cost-saving status. The break-even file quantifies the additional cost headroom before each strategy is no longer cost-saving.
