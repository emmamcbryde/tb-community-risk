# Milestone 3C decision register

## Potentially approvable

- Diagnostic one-scale inversion in a fully specified closed population.
- Reviewed compatible first-episode resident incidence and observation model.
- Complete natural history, explicit reviewed tail, risk effects and mortality.
- Verified grid-resolved identifiability, attainable target and numerical refinement.
- Explicit unresolved threshold approved for the exact configuration.

Synthetic fits demonstrate mathematics; they approve no empirical curve, legacy
RR or country inversion. No central tail or pediatric numerical default selected.

## Not approved

- Automatic inference from WHO incidence alone.
- Adult diagnostic surrogate as a national central curve: sensitivity only.
- Inversion with unresolved material children, unspecified tail or unreviewed effects.
- Ignoring migration/imported/recurrent disease, prior TB or historical depletion.
- Arbitrary caps; notification/prevalence/importation conversion; target clipping.
- Treating no extrapolation as zero hazard.
- Replacing dynamic transmission with fixed-pressure static inversion.
- Production availability or automatic source-mode fallback.

## Remains available

- User-specified overall, age-specific, age/sex and recent/remote TBI profiles.
- Subgroup-specific sources and population-weighted mixtures.
- Forward projection subject to evidence/coverage limits.
- Validation-only disagreement without altering user TBI.
- Explicit fictional/sensitivity diagnostics separated from empirical estimates.

## Further review and next milestone

Adult cause-specific curves, pediatric curves, remote tail, modern effect
definitions/estimands/treatment, licensed mortality, migration/importation,
recurrence/prior TB/depletion/selective survival, factor-specific association,
rare-group concentration limits and unresolved-threshold approval remain open.

Recommend **Milestone 3D: external scientific validation and observation
reconciliation**. Complete age/tail/effect evidence and licensed mortality;
independently validate compatible first-episode targets and approve/reject
precise configurations. Production UI, public inversion, interventions,
economics/DALYs/QALYs and dynamic integration remain deferred. No next-milestone
implementation is started automatically.
