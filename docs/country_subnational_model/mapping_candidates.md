# Candidate disease-incidence to pressure-shape mappings

All rows require the explicit forward disease/natural-history mapping in the
[scientific specification](calibration_specification.md) before claiming an
incidence-calibrated pressure history. Descriptive trend smoothing alone
does not provide that mapping. Absolute scale is separately calibrated to
reviewed TBI observations or supplied as uncertain assumptions. No Styblo
conversion or direct disease-rate substitution is allowed.

Common inputs are the immutable WHO ID/hash, rate and unchanged uncertainty
bounds, entity and years, reference normalization, natural-history/observation
policy, explicit prehistory and future-pressure policies. Parameters must be
constrained so pressure is finite/nonnegative and history covers every modelled
birth. Strictly positive log methods reject zeros; no arbitrary epsilon.
Where incidence is zero, consider a separately specified nonnegative model
with its observation process, or reject that method.

WHO annual values are modelled, correlated estimates, not 25 independent
Poisson counts. Their lower/upper envelopes are not an identified multivariate
distribution. For every candidate below, retain the published bounds and
compare coherent central/lower/upper shape scenarios; any probabilistic
correlation/distribution assumption must be explicit. Normalization removes
scale information from a flat envelope, so it cannot stand in for uncertainty
in absolute infection scale.

| Method | Inputs, equation and assumptions | Parameters/constraints and identifiability | Bounds and missing years |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proportional descriptor | \(r(t)\propto I(t)^\beta\); each annual value is a pressure-shape proxy only under a reviewed kernel/discrepancy scenario | Fix \(\beta\), normalize geometric mean on stated years; free \(k\) only initially. \(\beta=1\) is a strong scenario. Disease delay/composition changes can invalidate it | Coherent bound trajectories; do not independently perturb each year. Missing years fail unless a reviewed pressure bridge is selected and labelled modelled |
| Smoothed log-linear | Fit \(\log I=c+bt\) over an explicit window, then \(r=\exp[\beta b(t-t_{ref})]\). Supposes stable effective delay/progression/composition or quantified discrepancy | Fixed window, fit criterion, weights and \(\beta\); slope \(b\) conditional. \(c\) is not infection scale. Do not jointly identify free disease progression and \(k\) | Fit central and bound-trajectory slopes; weights not invented as inverse variances. Missing-year model predictions explicit, never inserted into WHO observations |
| Penalised spline | Fit positive log-shape with fixed knots and curvature penalty; use a named natural-history operator/discrepancy to relate disease timing | Knot locations, degree, penalty, boundary rules, normalization; penalty chosen/fixed independently of desired TBI fit. Flexible age/backcast/assay plus spline poorly identified | Refit coherent bound scenarios; modelled gaps flagged with extra uncertainty. No spline across territorial changes |
| State-space latent trend | Disease state generated by a progression/detection kernel; latent log-pressure follows local level/slope evolution; WHO estimates have an explicit correlated observation discrepancy | Initial level/slope, innovation scales, kernel, priors and observation covariance. Requires stronger evidence; latent pressure and disease/detection shocks confound | Bounds alone insufficient for likelihood. A stated measurement model and covariance sensitivity required. Missing observations omitted, latent states carry widening uncertainty |
| Piecewise/COVID-aware | Low-dimensional pre-disruption trend plus explicit 2020–2022 disease/detection/delay component; pressure response is separately specified | Break years, trend slopes and constrained shock/lag; do not infer a pressure dip merely from notification reduction. Avoid free shock in both pressure and detection | Retain all WHO values; compare no break vs labelled downweighted disruption vs reviewed shock model. Missing years never zero-filled |
| User-supplied pressure | Dated hazard/risk observations or reviewed historical reconstruction with units and uncertainty. Annual risk \(q\) converts via \(-\log(1-q)/\Delta t\), only for matched susceptible exposure intervals | Direct pressure evidence may inform scale; provenance, representativeness, age/sex transport and interpolation rules are reviewed. Incidence-only user data are not this mode | Preserve provided bounds and quantity; \(q=1\) has no finite constant hazard and fails. Missing history requires explicit joining/backcast, not implicit interpolation |

| Method | Before 2000 and after 2024 | 2020–2022 treatment | Failure modes, burden and use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proportional | Prehistory chosen separately; no automatic extrapolation. Post-2024 stop by default; explicit hold/continued trend/user curve only if requested | Raw disease disruption retained as a strong pressure-shape scenario; compare reviewed disruption alternatives | Misinterprets disease shocks as transmission; noisy recency. Very low burden; deterministic integration and stochastic hazards both possible |
| Log-linear | Explicit bounded backcast of early fitted slope or alternative policy. Future continuation requires approved horizon and bounds | Primary descriptor fits named pre-disruption window; compare all-years and segmented fits, not silent deletion | Misses reversals, confounds cohort and calendar trends; low burden. Both deterministic/stochastic use |
| Spline | No uncontrolled spline extrapolation; join a named bounded tail/backcast with continuity rule. Stop at 2024 without future policy | Fixed knots/penalty can oversmooth or reproduce disruption; compare explicit break | End oscillations, overfit, nonidentifiability; moderate burden, both uses after curve freezing |
| State-space | Prior initial state/backcast essential; future forecast uncertainty increases, not a new observation | Separate disease/detection shock supported by external data; pressure shock otherwise confounded | Prior-dominated latent scale, overconfidence, MCMC/convergence burden. Moderate/high burden; deterministic conditional means or explicit posterior draws, not interchangeable |
| Piecewise | Backcast earliest segment only with bounds; future last segment only under explicit policy | Explicit candidate break/bridge; data retained and residuals reported for every year | Country disruption differed; arbitrary break affects recent stock. Low/moderate burden; both uses |
| User pressure | Only supplied coverage; explicit earlier join and later projection needed | Retain real pressure evidence; do not overwrite to resemble disease | Assay conversion/selection bias, gaps and imported exposures; low burden for frozen curve, both uses |

**Provisional recommendation:** begin 2C with supplied synthetic pressure and
one-scale recovery. For a later empirical pilot, compare a parsimonious
log-linear descriptor and a fixed-break COVID-aware descriptor under reviewed
natural-history scenarios, against the unsmoothed proportional scenario.
Fix method/window/normalization before inspecting desired TBI fit. Do not
declare any of these a validated default country mapping in 2B. Splines and
state-space methods are deferred until residuals and independent evidence
justify additional parameters.

The COVID period affected disease and care processes; WHO explicitly monitors
notification disruption. See [WHO data definitions](https://www.who.int/teams/global-tuberculosis-programme/data/).
That supports treating notification and disease processes separately, not a
particular pressure-shock magnitude or date for any country.
