# Questions for the TSTin3D authors

Prepared for review; no message has been sent.

1. Can the smoking threshold (Table 2 >=1 pack/day versus Figure 1 >1) and
   exact two-year recency boundary be clarified?
2. Which exact source-code/parameter version corresponds to the 2008 paper,
   Figures 1-2 (screenshot Version 1.0, January19 2006) and current Version 3.0?
   Can immutable source snapshots, a change log and reference test vectors be supplied?
3. What are the original country/state incidence and NTM tables, data years,
   units and licensing? How do age at immigration and residence alter exposure?
4. How are size-specific BCG effects obtained from the aggregate published values?
   What changes explain the different current About values?
5. What is the general concurrent-risk-factor rule, including recency plus medical
   factors? Table 3's granuloma/underweight pair alone does not establish it.
6. How are the two-year recent-infection period, close-contact baseline and
   lifetime integration reconciled? Why do the printed contact results differ
   from both the literal lifetime equation and the Table 2 footnote schedule?
7. Can the Haiti >=15mm healthy/diabetic Table 3 values and Figure 2 rounding be
   reconciled with the printed PPVs? Were different unprinted inputs used?
8. What exactly happens for unknown, blank, excluded, negative, borderline,
   indeterminate and discordant tests? How are serial tests and prior treatment handled?
9. Are the reported medical effects crude/adjusted RRs, rate ratios, odds ratios
   or conditional progression estimates? What populations, treatments and horizons
   justify each selected value, especially transplant, HIV, silicosis and dialysis?
10. What are the age restrictions, age80/above80 rules, saturation/capping behaviour,
   ethnicity multiplier bounds and rounding/truncation conventions?
11. Which contemporary validations, calibration/fairness assessments and subgroup
    transport checks exist? What are the approved clinical-use and reuse limits?
12. What source/code/data licence, attribution and redistribution terms apply to
    an independent research reference implementation and to any future clinical use?
