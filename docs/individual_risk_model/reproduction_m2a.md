Table 3 granuloma/underweight cannot be reproduced with a verified general historical rule. For RR2 and3, modern maximum/additive-excess/multiplicative give3/4/6; the Florida >=15mm cell implies5. That single pair is insufficient to adopt a general sum rule.

The Canadian contact cell prints9.0% at age35 and PPV1. Literal ARD(.015)*45 yields67.5%; a hypothetical tau0 two-year footnote schedule gives7.3%. Neither equals9.0%. Current-site early-contact rules are not imported to force agreement.
